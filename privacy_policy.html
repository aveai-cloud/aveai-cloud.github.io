2 Information collected for general services

We collect information you provide, such as a wallet address, account or contact details, preferences, support enquiries and feedback. We also collect service usage information, including pages and features used, device and browser information, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, session identifiers and access times.

Public blockchain information, including wallet addresses, transaction hashes, amounts, network identifiers and transaction history, may be associated with your account or payment. A wallet address may constitute personal data when linked to an identifiable person.

3 Additional information for QR payments

When you scan or submit a supported QR code and initiate a payment, we process the code’s payment payload, merchant or payee identifiers, order or invoice reference, requested fiat amount and currency, USDT amount, selected blockchain network, applicable quote and fees, payment instructions, wallet or account reference, transaction hash, timestamps, status, reconciliation records and any refund or dispute information.

The platform receives the USDT you transfer for a payment. A third-party payment and settlement provider uses USDT pre-funded by the platform to arrange a fiat payment to the merchant. We process records that connect your payment instruction and incoming USDT transfer with the corresponding merchant settlement. This does not imply that the same on-chain units you send are transferred directly to the merchant.

Camera access may be requested to scan a QR code or capture verification information. If you choose an image-upload option, we process the image you select. Permissions can be managed in your device settings; disabling them may prevent the related feature from working. Payment scanning is distinct from biometric identity verification.

4 Identity verification and compliance

Where required for the service, we collect or receive identity details, contact and address information, date of birth, nationality or residence, identity documents, verification results and supporting due diligence information. Depending on the checks performed, this may include source-of-funds information, transaction explanations, beneficial ownership details, sanctions or politically exposed person screening results and risk review records.

Identity and liveness checks may require facial images, video or biometric data. We use Sumsub and other approved verification or screening providers for the checks enabled in the relevant flow. Their notices identify their processing, including any independent purposes. We present the required notices and obtain any required consent before data is submitted. Refusal may mean that we cannot enable or continue QR payments, but does not automatically prevent use of unrelated features.

5 How and why we use personal data

We use relevant data to deliver market information and requested features; establish and administer accounts; verify identity and eligibility; create and execute payment instructions; confirm receipt of USDT; communicate with payment providers; reconcile merchant settlement; investigate failed, duplicate or disputed payments; facilitate eligible refunds; and respond to support requests.

We also use necessary data to secure our systems, detect fraud and abuse, assess sanctions and other compliance risks, maintain required records, respond to lawful requests and establish or defend legal claims. Optional analytics and advertising are handled separately from essential service processing. We may use information that has been effectively anonymized for statistics and research.

The legal grounds depend on the processing and applicable law. They may include steps requested by you or performance of a contract where recognized, compliance with legal obligations, permitted legitimate interests or other statutory exceptions, and consent where required. We assess the applicable conditions before relying on any ground; these grounds are not interchangeable in every jurisdiction. Withdrawing optional consent does not remove a lawful obligation to retain or otherwise process particular records.

6 Who receives personal data

NEW DATA AI PTE LTD and authorized service personnel receive only the information necessary for their technology, verification administration, operational or support roles, under appropriate confidentiality and processing arrangements.

Identity verification, screening and fraud-prevention providers receive data needed to perform their checks and return results. Sumsub may also process data for independent purposes described in its notices. See https://sumsub.com/notification-to-processing-of-personal-data/ and the notices shown before verification.

Payment and settlement providers receive payment instructions, merchant or payee information, amounts, references and status information. Identity or compliance records are shared with them only where necessary for the relevant service or legal obligations and supported by a lawful basis. Merchants receive information needed to identify, fulfil, reconcile or refund a payment; identity documents and biometric data are not routinely supplied to merchants.

Other recipients may include hosting, infrastructure, security and customer-support providers; professional advisers; regulators, courts or law-enforcement bodies where disclosure is lawfully required or justified; and parties to a corporate reorganization subject to lawful safeguards. A provider acting for its own regulatory or service purposes may have its own privacy notice. We identify such providers and provide relevant notices where required, including in the payment or verification flow.

Public blockchain transactions are visible to network participants and potentially anyone using a blockchain explorer. We do not use public blockchains to publish your identity documents or biometric verification records.

9 Retention and blockchain records

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary for the disclosed purposes and applicable legal or justified business requirements. Account data is retained while the service relationship continues and for the necessary closure period. Payment, KYC and compliance records are retained for the applicable period measured from the relevant transaction, completion of verification or end of the relationship, as the governing requirement specifies.

Applicable retention schedule: account and support records 6 months; payment and reconciliation records 6 months; KYC, identity images and biometric information 6 months; security logs 6 months. Legal holds may extend a period for the affected records. We then securely delete or effectively anonymize the information, including through a controlled backup deletion cycle.

Deleting an account does not delete records that must lawfully be retained or transactions recorded on a public blockchain. We generally cannot alter or remove public blockchain records or copies independently held by network participants.
